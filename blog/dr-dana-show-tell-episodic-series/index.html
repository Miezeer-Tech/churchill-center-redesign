    Dr. Dana Show & Tell Episodic Series

    • Articles, Video Library

    • The Churchill Center

    As a Doctor of Functional and Naturopathic Medicine, he proves time and again that optimal health and wellbeing is achievable at any age! Simply said, Dr. Dana Churchill is the ‘real deal’. Dr. Dana Show & Tell features engaging subjects that will assure audiences at home and in studio, that a life of dignity and stress-free happiness is within reach for all! “It’s only natural”, Dr. Dana is fond of saying. He is joined by celebrated guests sharing interactive, immersive, and vetted stories that play out in-studio and in lush and verdant California habitats. ‘Show and Tell’focus on captivating current events that are meant to inspire dialogue rather than confrontation, subject that are like a rejuvenating breath of fresh air. Dr. Dana explores with his guests natural and effective solutions to ‘disease’. Dr. Dana Show & Tell promises compelling subject matter that entertains and enlightens while restoring grace, justice and a ‘Joie de vivre’ in a troubled world.
