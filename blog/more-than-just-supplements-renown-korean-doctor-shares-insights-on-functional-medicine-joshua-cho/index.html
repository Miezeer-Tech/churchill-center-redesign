    Chapter 8 – More Than Just Supplements: Renown Korean Doctor Shares Insights on Functional Medicine | Joshua Cho

    • Dr Dana Show
    • March 27, 2025
    • The Churchill Center

    Dr. Joshua Cho’s YouTube: / @drjoshuacho (opens in new tab) In this insightful interview, we sit down with Dr. Cho , a pioneering figure in the world of functional medicine and a respected voice in the Korean healthcare community. Dr. Cho’s journey is truly remarkable – starting as a chiropractor, he found his calling in personalized nutrition and holistic care after his father’s cancer diagnosis. Driven by a desire to provide his patients with more comprehensive and effective treatment options, Dr. Cho has become a trailblazer, building a thriving YouTube channel with over 400,000 followers and developing his own line of targeted nutritional supplements. But Dr. Cho’s impact extends far beyond his clinical practice. He has emerged as a passionate advocate, using his platform to educate the public on important health topics and challenge the status quo in the medical industry. From his insights on the pandemic to his views on the need for greater insurance coverage of alternative therapies, Dr. Cho’s perspectives offer a refreshing and thought-provoking counterpoint to the mainstream narrative. Join us as we delve into Dr. Cho’s inspiring story, his innovative approach to healthcare, and his mission to empower the Korean community and beyond. This conversation is a must-watch for anyone interested in the transformative power of functional medicine and the importance of holistic, patient-centered care.
