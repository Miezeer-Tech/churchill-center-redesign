    Dr. Dana Show (opens in new tab) hosted by the compassionate and renowned Dr. Dana Churchill, is a transformational interview podcast that spotlights individuals who have turned personal trauma into a catalyst for change. Featuring stories of resilience, breakthroughs, and divine inspiration, the show uncovers the suppressed truths about holistic health and well-being, while offering an uplifting message of hope, healing, and community impact. Dr. Dana’s empathetic approach as a licensed naturopathic doctor and functional medicine expert adds depth and authenticity to each episode, making “Epiphany” a trusted space for those seeking enlightenment, holistic healing, and actionable solutions for a better life.
