    Dr. Dana Churchill, NMD

    Esteemed throughout Southern California and beyond, Dr. Dana Churchill and his dedicated staff are sought out by the world’s most recognized celebrities. Each patient receives concierge style care and discreet treatments in the privacy of their home, office or at the Churchill Centers conveniently located practices throughout Los Angeles and Orange County.

    Established in 2005, The Churchill Center is a naturopathic medical clinic specializing in functional, anti-aging, and regenerative medicine. Led by Dr. Dana Churchill, N.M.D., we focus on identifying and healing the underlying root causes of physical, mental, and emotional symptoms rather than just treating surface issues.

    About The Churchill Center

    Dr. Churchill earned his Bachelor of Science in Nutritional Biochemistry from Rutgers University and his Doctor of Naturopathic Medicine from the National University of Natural Medicine (NUNM). His extensive training includes clinical experience at the Princeton Bio Center with orthomolecular pioneer Carl Pfeiffer, MD, PhD, five years collaborating with renowned homeopath Will Taylor, MD, over 15 years attending A4M Functional Medicine Congresses, and recent training with Dr. Thierry Hertoghe, MD, Kent Holtorf, MD and Dr. Dayan Goodenowe, PhD.

    With deep expertise in gut pathologies and re-building gut walls, stopping autoimmune attacks, thyroid disorders, mold toxicity, heart health, adrenal fatigue, hormone imbalances (menopause and andropause), chronic fatigue, fibromyalgia, leaky gut, allergies, and weight loss, Dr. Churchill creates highly personalized care plans.

    We integrate advanced IV therapies (Plaquex, glutathione, and EDTA chelation), bioidentical hormone replacement therapy (BHRT), peptides, regenerative medicine, homeopathy, botanical medicine, therapeutic diets, and nutritional support. Pharmaceuticals are used only as a last resort.

    In recent years, we have developed comprehensive Longevity programs featuring genetic testing, detoxification, inflammation reduction, and full organ system optimization over 3–12 months. These programs support both complex health conditions and aesthetic/anti-aging goals from the inside out.

    Every pharmacy, stem cell company, peptide supplier, and equipment provider we work with undergoes rigorous vetting. Dr. Churchill personally meets with the CEOs and owners to understand their motives and assess their integrity. With decades of hands-on experience using natural medicines, he has in-depth knowledge of brands and selects only those meeting the highest standards of quality, purity, and efficacy.

    Our goal is prevention, long-term wellness, and restoring vitality for patients in the Pasadena, Arcadia, and Monrovia areas.

    Dr. Julietta Thong, DAIM

    Certified Lymphatic & Integrative Wellness Therapy Specialist

    Dr. Julietta’s journey into healing began long before she entered a classroom. As a survivor of the Khmer Rouge era in Cambodia and later a refugee who rebuilt her life in the United States, she developed a deep appreciation for the resilience of the human body, the importance of hope, and the transformative power of healing.

    Read full bioShow less

    Driven by a lifelong passion for helping others, Dr. Julietta pursued advanced education in integrative health and wellness, earning a Doctor of Acupuncture and Integrative Medicine (DAIM), a Master of Science in Traditional Asian Medicine (MSTAM), a Doctorate in Biomedical Nutrition and Psychoneurology, and an MBA in Global Management. Her diverse educational background reflects her belief that true wellness is achieved by integrating the best of modern science with time-tested healing principles.

    Today, Dr. Julietta specializes in AcuRoma™ Wellness and Lymphatic Therapy, a unique approach that combines therapeutic bodywork, lymphatic drainage techniques, meridian-based principles, and relaxation-focused care to support circulation, fluid movement, recovery, and overall well-being.

    Her work focuses on three core services:

    • Wellness Maintenance Bodywork
    • Pre-Operative Lymphatic Preparation
    • Post-Operative Lymphatic Recovery

    Dr. Julietta’s philosophy is simple: support the body’s natural ability to heal, restore balance, and optimize wellness. Her treatments are designed to complement medical care while helping patients feel relaxed, supported, and empowered throughout their health journey.

    Patients appreciate her gentle approach, compassionate presence, and commitment to individualized care. Whether supporting recovery, enhancing wellness, or promoting relaxation, Dr. Julietta’s goal is to help each patient achieve greater comfort, vitality, and quality of life.

    Through her collaboration with The Churchill Center, Dr. Julietta is honored to support individuals in achieving optimal wellness, recovery, and long-term vitality through compassionate, integrative care.
