    Overview

    Women have distinct health concerns, and some conditions that affect both men and women can impact women differently. Beyond the more apparent issues such as pregnancy, menopause, and disorders of the female reproductive system, Churchill Centers™ emphasizes the importance of preventive screenings for breast cancer, cervical cancer, heart disease, diabetes, thyroid disorders, sub-clinical hypothyroidism, severe menopausal symptoms, and bone density. These screenings, along with comprehensive preventive care, are crucial for maintaining overall health. The human body is inherently resilient, and many serious diseases can be mitigated, prevented, or alleviated through proactive health measures.

    Read moreShow less

    Men and women share many common health issues, but these conditions can affect women in distinct ways. For instance:

    • Women are more likely to die following a heart attack than men
    • Women are more likely to show signs of depression and anxiety than men
    • The effects of sexually transmitted diseases can be more serious in women
    • Osteoarthritis affects more women than men
    • Women are more likely to have urinary tract problems
